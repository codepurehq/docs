Codepure is built to help organizations worldwide meet their strictest regulatory requirements — whether you operate in North America, Europe, the Gulf, or anywhere else.

## Framework Alignment
By hosting your data securely and giving you deep visibility into your code vulnerabilities, Codepure is designed to help your organization work toward the requirements of the frameworks that matter most to you, including:

* **Global & Europe:** GDPR (EU/EEA data protection) and the SOC 2 Trust Services Criteria for security, availability, and confidentiality.
* **United States:** Frameworks such as SOC 2 and NIST secure-development guidance.
* **Middle East & GCC:** NCA ECC-1:2024 and SAMA (Saudi Arabia), NESA and DESC (UAE), NCSA NIA (Qatar), and local central-bank and government mandates (Oman, Kuwait).
* **Industry:** PCI DSS for payment-handling applications.

Codepure aligns with and is designed to support these frameworks, mapping findings to relevant controls to simplify your audits. Codepure is a tool that assists your compliance program; it is not a substitute for certification or independent legal advice.

## Secure by Design
We do not permanently store your source code. Our "scan and discard" architecture ensures that your intellectual property never becomes a liability, simplifying your compliance audits drastically.
