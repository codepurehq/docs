When you use Codepure to scan your applications, you are trusting us with your most valuable asset. We designed our infrastructure so your data stays secure, wherever your organization operates.

## 1. Where Your Data Is Hosted
Codepure SaaS is hosted in the **United States** on enterprise-grade cloud infrastructure. This is the default hosting location for the platform.

* **Cloud Provider:** Google Cloud Platform (GCP).
* **Data Guarantee:** Your account data, scan metadata, and vulnerability reports stay within the platform's hosted environment.

### GCC / Middle East Residency (On Request)
For customers who are specifically required to keep data within the Gulf region, Codepure offers **GCC data residency on request** to help meet local data-sovereignty requirements. This is available on request rather than as a self-serve option — contact us at admin@codepure.com to discuss your requirements.

## 2. The "Zero Storage" Source Code Policy
* **What we do:** When you trigger a scan, Codepure pulls your code into a temporary, isolated memory environment. We scan it, generate the report, and instantly destroy the environment.
* **What we DO NOT do:** We never permanently store, copy, or back up your proprietary source code.
