When you use Codepure to scan your applications, you are trusting us with your most valuable asset. We designed our infrastructure so your data stays secure and aligned with the data sovereignty requirements of your region — wherever your organization operates.

## 1. Choose Your Region
Codepure offers regional data residency so your account data, scan metadata, and vulnerability reports stay within the cloud region you select. Hosting options are available across:

* **Middle East / Gulf** — for organizations with GCC data-sovereignty requirements (e.g., Saudi Arabia, UAE, Qatar, Oman, Kuwait).
* **United States** — for North American teams.
* **Europe** — for organizations with EU/EEA data-protection requirements.

* **Cloud Provider:** Google Cloud Platform (GCP), using enterprise-grade regional data centers.
* **Data Guarantee:** Your account data, scan metadata, and vulnerability reports stay within the cloud region you choose. Need a specific region? Contact us at fahad@codepure.com.

## 2. The "Zero Storage" Source Code Policy
* **What we do:** When you trigger a scan, Codepure pulls your code into a temporary, isolated memory environment. We scan it, generate the report, and instantly destroy the environment.
* **What we DO NOT do:** We never permanently store, copy, or back up your proprietary source code.
